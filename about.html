About Me

A little about where I come from, what I'm learning, and the kind of engineer I hope to become.

I'm Munaf Abid, a Software Engineering student at NED University of Engineering & Technology. I chose this field because I like understanding how things work behind the screen, not just using apps and websites. I'm especially interested in problem-solving, programming logic, and the way a well-written program can make a task easier.

I'm still at the beginning of my university journey, so I see every class, assignment, and small project as a chance to improve. I don't expect to know everything straight away. My focus is to build strong fundamentals, practise consistently, learn from mistakes, and gradually take on more challenging work.

In the long run, I'd like to grow into a capable software engineer with a good understanding of both front-end development and back-end systems. For now, I'm concentrating on learning the basics properly and making steady progress.
